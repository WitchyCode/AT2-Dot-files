# AT2-Dot-files
Personal linux rice themed after the video game Ar Tonelico 2: Melody of Metafalica

Very much a WIP

# Design Specifications
* Heavy Use of transparency effects to give a glass-like look to windows and bars
* Colour scheme consisting mainly of Greens/Blues/Purples
* Clean, futuristic aesthetics with an emphasis on technology or musical themes
* Clean minimalist wallpaper related to AT2, designed by myself
* Tasteful use of animations and sound effects that mimic the game's look and feel
